FAQ

Questions, answered

Will my clients know you exist?

No. I never contact your clients, never post their videos and never put my name, watermark or credit on an edit. You deliver the work as your own.

Can you sign our NDA?

Yes. Send it with your first clip and I will sign it before I open the footage.

Can you match our house style?

Yes. Send your style guide, or three reels you have already posted for a client. I copy the captions, fonts, colours, pacing and end card, and save it as a brand kit for every edit after.

How do we send footage and notes?

Google Drive, Dropbox or Frame.io for footage. Notes by email or in Frame.io comments. Finished files come back in the same folder.

What is the turnaround?

48 hours a reel. I work on India time (IST), so footage you send at the end of your day in the UK, US, Australia or the UAE is often edited by your next morning.

What do you deliver?

MP4s in the sizes you need (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9), captions as an SRT file, and the project file on request.

What if we need more than 20 reels a month?

Ask me for a quote. Plans can go up when you win a client and down in a quiet month, with 14 days' notice.

What are the examples on this page?

Spec edits. I cut them from agencies' and creators' public videos to show my work. Each card says where the footage came from. None of it is paid client work.

How do we pay?

Monthly in advance by Wise or PayPal. The test edit is free and needs no card. If you are not happy in the first 14 days, you get your money back.
