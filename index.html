07 / FAQ

Questions.

What do I need to send?

Raw clips in a Google Drive or Dropbox folder, your logo or fonts if you have them, and one reel you like the style of.

How fast is it?

48 hours for each reel. On monthly plans I batch your reels so you always have the week's posts ready.

Can you work under my agency's name?

Yes. White-label is normal for me. Your clients only see your brand.

What time zone are you in?

India (IST). I work with clients in the UK, Australia, Canada and the UAE, so your edits are often ready when you wake up.

How do I pay?

Monthly in advance by card or bank transfer (Wise or PayPal). The test edit is free.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Once a month is paid, every edit and project file from it is yours to use anywhere.

What if I don't like an edit?

Two rounds of changes on every reel. If you're still not happy in the first 14 days, you get your money back.
