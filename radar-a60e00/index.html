Why Foxconn India Got Dragged to Insolvency Court

“₹143 crore. Foxconn makes your ₹1.3 lakh iPhone, but they just got dragged to insolvency court for unpaid rent.”

Apple's primary manufacturer, Foxconn, faces an insolvency petition at NCLT Chennai filed by Bengaluru landlord BCD Emprasa. In April 2024, Foxconn leased 700 residential units to house female factory workers under a lock-in agreement through July 2026. The landlord claims Foxconn stopped clearing monthly rent invoices in May 2025 and now owes ₹143 crore in dues. While Apple champions its massive Indian expansion, local real estate vendors say multinational bills are piling up unpaid.

India India promotes itself as the premier alternative to China for global tech manufacturing, but dragging Apple's top maker to bankruptcy court exposes serious vendor payment frictions.

At home Your next iPhone still costs ₹1.3 lakh, but the local Indian vendors housing the workers who build it are fighting in court for basic rent.

Joke Foxconn builds phones with satellite tracking and microchips, but their accounting desk apparently lost the landlord's UPI address 18 months ago.

Risk The NCLT could quickly dismiss the petition as a standard contractual lease dispute rather than a genuine corporate insolvency.

Why Bank Workers Are Striking Over IDBI's Sale

“60.72% of IDBI Bank is being sold to a Canadian billionaire, and bank employees are walking out next Tuesday.”

The Indian government and LIC approved selling their combined 60.72% controlling stake in IDBI Bank to Canada's Fairfax for ₹52,878 crore. LIC originally rescued the struggling lender with policyholder funds back in 2019 when bad loans spiked past 27%. Now that the bank is profitable again with bad loans down to 3.6%, the state is handing control to foreign investors. Opposing the privatization, nationwide bank employee unions have called for a massive strike on December 3.

India This deal represents the first time India is selling a state-rescued public lender to a foreign financial conglomerate, setting a contentious precedent for PSU banks.

At home If your family keeps an IDBI Bank account, branch counters will shut next Tuesday, and your safe public deposit shifts to a private foreign owner.

Joke LIC used public insurance money to nurse the sick bank back to health, and the second it recovered, they shipped it off to Canada.

Risk Indian bank employee unions frequently call nationwide strikes that get called off at the last minute after government mediation talks.
